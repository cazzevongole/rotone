RoTone rotates the sound your phone plays for notifications.

Put any number of notification ringtones — or your own audio files — into a list, arrange them in the order you want (or switch to shuffle), and every time a notification plays RoTone sounds the next tone and moves the pointer on, looping back to the first at the end.

Rotations can be global or per app: give your chat app its own list, switch rotation off for the bank, and let everything else share the default rotation — each list keeps its own pointer.

How it works

Android does not let one app change another app's notification sound. RoTone works with the platform instead:

1. You grant it notification access, so it can see when a notification is posted.
2. When one arrives, RoTone plays the next tone in your rotation itself, on the notification audio stream, so the notification volume and silent mode apply.
3. To hear only RoTone, set the apps you care about to Silent once, in their own notification settings — otherwise their normal sound plays on top of the rotating tone.

That third step is the only manual setup, and it is a one-time job per app.

Features

* In-order and shuffle rotation, both looping forever
* Per-app rotations, each with its own list, mode and pointer
* Import any system ringtone or any audio file; tones are copied into the app so they keep working
* Pause switch, restart button and a preview on every row
* Silent, vibrate and Do Not Disturb are respected
* English and Italian, following the system language
* No internet permission, no ads, no tracking, no accounts
